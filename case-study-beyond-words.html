Beyond Words

A real-time sound awareness system for hearing-impaired individuals.

Problem

Important environmental sounds are invisible to hearing-impaired individuals.

Goal

Translate critical sounds into tactile and visual feedback in real-time.

My Role

UI/UX Designer, Prototyper

Duration

12 Weeks

Tools Used

Figma, Arduino, React Native

"What happens when important sounds become invisible?"

For hearing-impaired individuals, everyday environments can be stressful due to the inability to perceive auditory cues like fire alarms, approaching vehicles, or doorbells.

Research & Discovery

User Interviews

Conducted interviews with 15 hearing-impaired individuals to understand their daily challenges in public and private spaces.

Accessibility Insights

Found that existing solutions are often bulky, expensive, or fail to differentiate between urgent and non-urgent sounds.

Real-World Observations

Observed high cognitive load when users navigate busy streets, relying entirely on visual scanning.

Secondary Research

Analyzed current wearable tech and haptic feedback patterns to identify gaps in continuous environmental monitoring.

Under the Hood

Hardware

  • Arduino UNO / Nano for prototyping
  • Coin vibration motors for localized haptics
  • Bluetooth LE for smartphone syncing

Software

  • TensorFlow Lite for audio classification
  • React Native for cross-platform app
  • Real-time WebSockets for instant alerts

Impact & Future

Accessibility Impact

Significantly reduced cognitive load and anxiety for users navigating urban environments independently.

Future Scalability

Integration with smartwatch OS natively, and personalized AI training for recognizing specific voices (like family members).
