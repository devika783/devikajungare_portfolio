About Me

“I’m Devika — a UI/UX and interaction designer crafting immersive, emotionally-driven digital experiences that balance aesthetics, accessibility, and storytelling.”

My work blends research, spatial thinking, motion, interaction, and strong visual systems to create experiences that feel intuitive, human, and memorable. From accessibility-focused systems and immersive cultural installations to speculative interfaces and environment-first narratives, I enjoy designing experiences that go beyond screens and create real emotional impact.

I’m deeply drawn towards bold ideas, cinematic presentation, thoughtful interaction, and high-detail execution. I love exploring the space where technology, human behavior, visual storytelling, and experiential design intersect.

Currently focused on building immersive interfaces, accessibility-driven systems, interaction-heavy products, and visually rich case studies with strong conceptual depth.

Always experimenting.
Always building.
Always designing with intent.
