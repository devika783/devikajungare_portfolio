Metro Accessibility Redesign

Designing safer and more inclusive metro experiences for senior citizens and specially-abled passengers.

  • ⚠ Straps are too high for many passengers
  • ⚠ Hard to balance during sudden brakes

  • ⚠ Isolation & lack of handles near coach ends

Accessibility Challenges

Stability During Movement

Lack of rigid support structures causes instability during acceleration and braking.

Limited Physical Support

Absence of armrests makes sitting down and standing up difficult for the elderly.

Unsafe Standing Posture

Current hanging straps do not offer ergonomic wrist positioning.

Seating Discomfort

Slippery surfaces and poor back support cause fatigue on longer journeys.

Poor Grip Accessibility

Handles are often out of reach for people with limited mobility or height.

Public Behavior Issues

Rush-hour crowds often block access to priority areas and support beams.

User-Centered Research

Analyzing behavioral patterns, accessibility pain points, and inclusive mobility needs.
