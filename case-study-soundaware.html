SoundAware

A real-time sound awareness and haptic feedback system for hearing-impaired individuals.

Problem

Crucial environmental sounds are inaccessible to hearing-impaired individuals.

Goal

Translate auditory alerts into visual and haptic feedback instantly.

My Role

UX Designer, Prototyper

Duration

8 Weeks

Tools Used

Figma, Arduino, C++

"What happens when important sounds become invisible?"

For the hearing-impaired, public spaces can be overwhelming when critical auditory cues like sirens or announcements are missed. This project explores bridging that sensory gap through technology.

Research & Insights

User Interviews

Spoke with 12 individuals to understand their emotional and physical challenges in public spaces.

Accessibility Insights

Current accessibility devices are heavily medicalized, creating stigma. Users want premium, discrete tech.

Public Alert Challenges

Visual alerts (flashing lights) in public are often missed if the user isn't looking directly at them.

Real-world Observations

High cognitive fatigue results from constant visual scanning of the environment.
