Context: Jan Aushadhi Kendra

The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) provides quality generic medicines at affordable prices. However, reaching these centers and finding consistent stock remains a barrier for many.

Myth

Generic medicines are less effective and of lower quality than branded ones.

Reality

Generic medicines follow the same strict quality guidelines and are equally effective, just more affordable.

"In public spaces, access to essential over-the-counter medicines is still unsafe, inaccessible, and inconsistent—especially for people with disabilities, the elderly, and daily commuters. Physical barriers, lack of clear visual guidance, poor communication support, and absence of reliable identity or usage tracking make medicine access confusing and risky."
